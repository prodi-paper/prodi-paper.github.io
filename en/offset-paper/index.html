FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is the difference between offset paper and coated paper?

Offset paper is uncoated: its surface is matte and absorbent, ideal for text, books and inserts. Coated paper receives a mineral coating that makes it smooth and glossy, better suited to images and magazines. Choose offset for readability and writing, coated for photographic rendering.

Which offset paper grammages do you have in stock?

We cover the full range, from light bulky book paper (around 60–70 gsm) up to heavy grammages and bristol (up to 300 gsm), including the standard 80/90/100 gsm. Tell us your grammage and reel width or sheet size and we check immediate availability.

Do you sell offset paper in reels or in sheets?

Both. We supply offset paper in reels (for web presses and converting) and in sheets on pallets (for sheet-fed printing). Cutting to a specific size is possible on request.

Do you export offset paper to Africa and internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide from France, mainly by container, with a strong focus on North, West, Central and East Africa and the Middle East. We provide the invoice, packing list and certificate of origin, and quote EXW, FOB or CIF depending on your logistics.

How fast can offset paper be shipped?

Most of our offset papers are physically in stock: shipment leaves within days once the offer is confirmed. That is our main advantage over a mill order, which usually takes several weeks of production.
