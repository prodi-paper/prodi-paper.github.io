Our story

More than 30 years exporting paper and board, from the Maghreb to West Africa and worldwide.

INTERNATIONAL PAPER & BOARD MERCHANT

Founded in 1991, Prodiconseil specialises in exporting paper and board to international markets — expertise built over more than 30 years in the industry.

The company grew around a hands-on knowledge of the paper industry and a network of reliable suppliers, in Europe and across the world.

For more than 30 years, clients and partners have trusted us for the quality of our products, the availability of our stock and our command of logistics constraints.

We supply paper and board for printing, writing, packaging, hygiene and technical applications — in reels or sheets, from one-off needs to industrial volumes.

Our international presence

Since 1991, Prodiconseil has supplied printers, converters and traders worldwide — from the Maghreb to all of Africa, the Middle East, Eastern Europe and South America.
