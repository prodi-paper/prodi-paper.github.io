FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you deliver paper to Lagos and other Nigerian ports?

Yes. We ship by container from France to Lagos Apapa and Tin Can Island, and can also route cargo via Lekki Deep Sea Port or Onne (Port Harcourt) depending on your forwarder. From the port, our customers distribute to Lagos, Ibadan, Abuja, Kano and Port Harcourt.

Which paper grades are available for the Nigerian market?

Offset paper, newsprint, coated paper, kraft and packaging board, as well as cut-size office paper in reams. Everything is available in reels and sheets from our permanent stock of more than 10,000 tonnes in France.

What documents do you provide for import into Nigeria?

We provide the commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin with every shipment. As the importer, you handle your local formalities with your clearing agent — we provide the export documents your clearing agent needs.

Which incoterms do you quote for Nigeria?

Depending on your logistics: EXW (ex warehouse), FOB French port or CIF destination port. We advise the most suitable incoterm for your shipment.

How do I get a price for paper shipped to Nigeria?

Send us the grade, grammage, reel width or sheet size and the quantity you need, by WhatsApp or email. We reply with an offer and the stock availability, usually within 24 hours.
